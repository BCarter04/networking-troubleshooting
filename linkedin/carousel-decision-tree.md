# Carousel: How I Diagnose “No Internet” in Under 2 Minutes

**Upload:** `carousel-decision-tree.pdf` as a Document post

**Caption**

When someone says the internet is down, I do not start checking things at random.

I use this simple decision tree. It points me at the right guide and the right PowerShell script in under two minutes.

Swipe to follow the path.

---

**Slide reference**

1. Cover — How I Diagnose “No Internet” in Under 2 Minutes
2. Can the machine reach anything? → basic-connectivity
3. Names fail but IPs work? → DNS
4. Only one site or port fails? → port-firewall
5. No IP or 169.254? → DHCP
6. Wi-Fi or slow/intermittent? → matching guide
7. One-command start — Get-NetworkSummary.ps1
8. Call to action — Full tree and scripts
